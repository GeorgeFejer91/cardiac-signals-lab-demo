# Reused social-avatar face assets

The seven files under `spherical/` are copied from the project-authored facial-expression library in `GeorgeFejer91/minimal-social-threat-webxr` and remain covered by that repository's MIT license. They are transparent 2:1 equirectangular SVG maps aligned to the procedural head's forward axis.

They are wrapped over the simplified 3D avatar heads and change with the illustrated round phase. They are evidence-grounded design prototypes, not normed or independently validated emotion stimuli. The two older root-level planar SVG files are retained only for source compatibility and are not used by the current WebGL widgets.
