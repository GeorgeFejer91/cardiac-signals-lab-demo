# Reused social-avatar face assets

`neutral.svg` and `happiness.svg` are copied from the project-authored facial-expression library in `GeorgeFejer91/minimal-social-threat-webxr` and remain covered by that repository's MIT license.

They are used here only to give the schematic tabletop avatars a consistent visual identity. They are evidence-grounded design prototypes, not normed or independently validated emotion stimuli.
