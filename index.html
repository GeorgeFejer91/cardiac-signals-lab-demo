Hidden Target

Goal: Choose the same target card without speaking.
  1. Player A privately sees which card is the target.
  2. Player A places one signal card. Its red edge pulses with Player A’s heartbeat.
  3. Player B chooses a target card. Both cards are revealed and points are awarded.

CooperatingBoth players win when Player B finds the correct target.

CompetingPlayer A wins by misleading Player B; Player B wins by finding the target.

Recorded outcomesTarget accuracy, decision time, cue use, payoff, and cardiac coupling.

The pulse is a cardiac-state cue. It is not labelled as confidence, truth, or deception.
